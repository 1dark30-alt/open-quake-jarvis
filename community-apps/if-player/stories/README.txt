Put story files in this folder.

Supported: Z-code (.z3 .z5 .z8 .zblorb) and Glulx (.ulx .gblorb) -- which covers
essentially everything Inform 6 and Inform 7 produce.

Any file you drop here appears on the app's story picker. You can also point the
page's "Story file" option straight at a filename in here, or at a full
http(s):// URL.

Where to find games: the IF Archive (ifarchive.org) and IFDB (ifdb.org) host
thousands of freely available works, including most competition entries. Check
each work's own terms before redistributing it.

No stories are bundled with this app, because their authors hold the rights to
them -- Infocom titles such as Zork are still under copyright and are NOT free
to redistribute.
